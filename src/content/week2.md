## Sept 16, 2026 — Full Group

Group meeting intent was to clarify which of our ideas to go with. 

- Group decided that our focus should be on designing a robotic device that targets the issues of habit forming, productivity, and reminders.
- The ideas in this space we currently are considering are: the robotic lamp, and a bipedal wheeled robot.
- Focus in the next meeting will be picking the final idea and planning from there. 

## Sept 25, 2026 — Full Group

Group meeting with focus on picking our favourite idea.

- Group decided to go with the idea of the lamp, based on the fact that it works to solve our favourite problem of human attention in a simpler way than the bipedal wheeled robot.
- The group decided to come up with a list of what we think the robot should do and use that collectively to develop the more refined constraints and criteria.
- The group also decided to each find one patent and one research paper to meet the next courde requirement.

## Sep 25-27, 2026 - Full Group

Each group member conducted research on productivity, mental health, and robotics, examining at least one relevant paper per topic.

Each group member developed a list of desired features for the robot.

## Sept 27, 2026 - Full Group

Group meeting with the focus of sharing our research findings and desired features. 

- Our research collectively indicated that keeping a phone out of reach—and ideally out of sight—is an effective strategy for reducing distractions and improving productivity.
- We found that cooler lighting can promote attention and alertness.
- We reviewed the Apple ELEGNT paper and accompanying video that originally inspired our project. The robot uses different task modes, which we agreed is a concept worth preserving in our design.
- The group is currently leaning toward incorporating the following peripherals: camera, microphone, speaker, lighting, and projector. We will also likely require an accompanying GUI or mobile/desktop application.
- Prepared slides for the upcoming meeting with our supervisors.
