## Oct. 16, 2026 — Brendan Chharawala

PDP slide work.

- Converted Shabd's project timeline into a Gantt chart so it would read clearly on the slides, keeping the content the same.
- Added more of our early research on how lighting affects focus and studying. I felt it was underrepresented in the slides, and accounting for it widens the lamp's lead in the design selection matrix.
- Discussion regarding using torque-feedback servos as the default. I advocated for this as torque feedback lets a user reposition the lamp by hand without the motors fighting them, and makes learned motions smoother and less robotic. A sensorless approach is possible, but it adds difficulty and project risk to save a few dollars per motor. This is based on two years of working with robot limbs on a previous project.
